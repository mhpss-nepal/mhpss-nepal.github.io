Daily / periodic MHPSS activity report

One row per activity, per site, per day. Do not add subtotal or summary rows — totals are calculated in the dashboard, never entered here.

This form records services, not people. Never enter a beneficiary's name, phone number, date of birth, identity number, diagnosis, assessment score, or any detail that could identify an individual. There is no field for it, and none is to be added. If someone needs individual follow-up, refer them — do not record them here.
Works offline. Reports are saved in this browser as you submit them. Nothing is sent anywhere. When you have signal, export the file and send it to the coordination focal point.
