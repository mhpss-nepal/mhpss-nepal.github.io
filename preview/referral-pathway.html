The GBV and child protection referral pathway, as it would appear

For discussion with UNICEF and UNFPA, 1 October 2026. The services below are read from the Nepal Protection Cluster’s province workbooks; only those that provide or carry mental health or psychosocial support are shown.

What would be shown, and what never would

Shown for each service

  • Organisation
  • Palika and district (a shelter or safe house: district only)
  • Service category, and the service description as entered
  • Whether psychosocial support is the service itself or part of another service
  • Opening hours, languages, accessibility
  • Who the service is for
  • The month the entry was last updated on the pathway

Never shown

  • Names of staff or focal points
  • Telephone numbers and email addresses
  • Street addresses and wards
  • The location of a shelter or safe house below its district
  • Anything about a person who uses a service

To reach a service, the page sends readers to the helplines and to the Protection Cluster’s own referral pathway.

How a service is selected — a service is included when its category is psychosocial support or psychological first aid, or when its own description mentions psychosocial, mental health or counselling support (legal and safety counselling excluded). This is a screening of the words used, not a check of quality or of IASC standards.
