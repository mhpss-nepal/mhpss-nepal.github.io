Layer 3 · Public

The layer that outlives the deployment

This page exists instead of a dashboard, deliberately. Layer 3 is the only layer that is meant to be permanent, so the first thing built is not the page — it is the agreement about who owns it. Nothing here is live yet.

Status

Not built   No public page exists. No figure from this response has been submitted into the national 5W. The submission itself is blocked by an unresolved question — see below.

What Layer 3 is

Two things, both owned by the Government of Nepal:

  1. Submission into the national 5W. NDRRMA already runs a 5W in which Protection–MHPSS is one of fourteen clusters. Mental health does not need a new instrument; it needs to appear in the one that exists. Registering as MHPSS information-management focal point and submitting is the whole task.
  2. A public page hosted by EDCD. Aggregated, service-level, publishable figures — which services are available where, and which holding centres have none. Hosted on the Ministry's own account, not ours.

What goes public, and what never does

PublishableNever published
Which services are available at which siteAnything identifying an individual
Coverage: which roster sites have no serviceNames, phone numbers, email addresses — of service users and of staff
Aggregate reach, disaggregated by sex and age bandPrecise location combined with a specific target group and a small count
Which organisations are working in which districtAny clinical detail, assessment score or diagnosis
The reporting instrument and the code listsFree-text descriptions that name a person

The third row on the right is the one people miss. In a displacement setting a precise location plus a specific target group becomes identifying even with no names attached — “three people in this target group at this coordinate on this date” can be enough. That is why location is recorded at coded site level and no finer, and why no GPS is collected anywhere in the system.

What blocks submission right now

The 5W attributes 4,847 people reached to Protection–MHPSS and 4,012 to Health. The mental health reporting sheet counts service contacts, not unique people — one person attending on three days counts three times. The figures are not comparable, and none of them is wrong; they count different things over different periods for different partner sets, and the same person can appear under both clusters.

Until there is a written ruling from NDRRMA and EDCD on contacts versus people reached, no figure from the mental health sheet goes into the 5W. Submitting the larger number as “people reached” would overstate the response, and it would be our overstatement.

Handover — the mechanism, not the intention

“We will hand it over” is not a plan. This is the plan:

  • The demonstration lives in a GitHub organisation, not a personal account, because an organisation can add owners and be transferred. A personal account cannot. GitHub's own guidance is to keep at least two owners; with one, the projects become inaccessible if that person is unreachable.
  • Handover is add an owner, then remove an owner — there is no password to pass on, because an organisation has no login of its own.
  • A named secretariat in writing, not a person who happens to hold the credentials.
  • One full update cycle run by the owner before the handover is called done. A system that has only ever been updated by its author has not been handed over; it has been abandoned politely.

Open in writing

The procurement route for a domain is one of three governance questions raised on 14 September 2026 and still unanswered. It matters here: a public page under a Ministry domain reads as the Ministry's; a page under anyone else's does not, whatever the footer says.
