Three layers, one direction

A national 5W already exists. It is run by NDRRMA, it is government-owned, and Protection–MHPSS is already one of its clusters. The mental health side runs a separate reporting sheet that does not feed it. So the gap is not the instrument. The gap is the connection.

What each layer does

Layer 1 · Field

Reporting

One row per activity, per site, per day, submitted by the organisation that delivered it. Coded sites, activities and organisations. Works offline, because upper Rasuwa is helicopter-only and is not expected to change for two to three months — a tool that needs a connection is a tool that does not reach the north at all.

No beneficiary fields exist and none are to be added.

Layer 2 · Coordination

Analysis

Coverage gaps, the duplication check, the 4Ws matrix, the deployed workforce. Restricted to the Technical Working Group. Holds service-level information — which organisation provides which service where — and never individual-level information.

Enforcement sits at the database, not in the page. A gate written in a web page is decoration: anyone can read the page's own source.

Layer 3 · Public

National instruments

Aggregated figures submitted into the NDRRMA 5W, and a public page owned and hosted by the Ministry through EDCD. This is the layer that outlives the deployment, which is why ownership of it is settled first and built second.

The boundary is a design rule, not a setting

Layer 1 writes. Layer 2 reads Layer 1 and writes nothing back. Layer 3 receives aggregates and cannot read the layer below it. No layer can reach down. That is what makes it safe to hand the public layer to the Ministry without handing over anything that identifies a person.

The question that decides everything

“Who holds the database” is three different questions, and conflating them is how projects like this fail quietly. Separate them and each has a defensible answer:

QuestionWhat it actually asksPosition
Who hosts?Whose infrastructure are the bytes physically on? WHO-provisioned infrastructure for Layers 1–2. Not a personal account — see below.
Who controls?Who grants and revokes access, and who can change the schema? A named secretariat, in writing. Not a person who happens to have the password.
Who owns?Whose data is it, and who decides what is published? The Government of Nepal. Publication is classified by the head of office, not by whoever wrote the page.

Why a personal account is not an option for real data

Placing WHO personal data with a third-party provider is a transfer requiring a written agreement with specified security undertakings. A personal account has none. A form with no backend cannot breach that rule, which is why the demonstration form has no backend. When a backend is authorised, only two functions in store.js change — the record shape, the deterministic id and the validation stay as they are.

There is a live precedent worth knowing: the Ministry of Finance publishes a Rasuwa flood update on GitHub Pages, so the pattern is accepted government practice in Nepal. It also sits on a personal account, which is precisely the ownership failure mode this architecture avoids — an organisation account can add owners and be transferred; a personal account cannot.

Sequencing — ownership before infrastructure

Stage
0Fix the formThe revised instrument on the government's own matrix. No infrastructure.
1Offline demonstratorWhat you are looking at. Synthetic data, running from a laptop.
2Proof of conceptReal submissions from a few partners, on WHO-provisioned infrastructure, for one cycle. Then a decision.
3Government handoverPublic layer at EDCD, a named secretariat in writing, one full update cycle run by the owner.

The order is deliberate. Building infrastructure first and finding an owner later is how a system becomes a personal project that dies when one person's contract ends.

Where this stands

Three questions were raised on 14 September 2026 and remain open in writing: hosting a prototype on a non-WHO account, AI assistance in development, and the procurement route for a domain. Until each has a written answer, this remains a demonstration running on synthetic content only.
